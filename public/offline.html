If this is an emergency

  • Call 911 or Burning Man emergency services.
  • Find the On Road Care number on the key tag.
  • El Monte RV cannot provide on-site service inside Black Rock City. Support there is operational guidance by phone only.

Propane smell or an alarm sounding, right now

  1. Put out any open flame and check every stove and oven knob is off.
  2. Turn off the house battery / battery disconnect switch.
  3. Open the windows, roof vents and entry door. Ventilation comes first — this is the one time the playa dust guidance does not apply.
  4. Go outside and close the propane shut-off valve on the tank.
  5. Everyone leaves the area until the smell has gone.
